## Decision

1. **Not enough shadow data.** The section 1 targets are confirmed "after the first shadow-mode week"; there is one day and two turns. Keep the router in `shadow` in this project until there are 7 days and 50 turns.
2. **Golden-set economics pass.** On 15 items the router is 41% cheaper than all-Opus at equal or better judged quality. The sample is small and the two judges agreed on 75% of re-judged rows, so this is a signal, not proof. All-Sonnet scored 0.94 for $0.28 on the same items, so the bar to beat may be Sonnet rather than Opus.
3. **Latency fails by design.** ScaleDown took 1.3 s and 1.6 s on the two live turns, and the hook fires only ~0.2 s before the first request. Shadow mode never waits, so nobody feels this today; in `active` mode every prompt would wait about 1 s. The owner accepted ScaleDown's latency for the POC (tdd-changes #4, #5). Before T6.2, choose one:
   - accept the wait and raise `max_added_latency_p50_ms` to 1500;
   - or have `active` mode forward without waiting and apply the decision from the next request of the turn (tool-use follow-ups), keeping the first request on the session's current model.
4. **Thresholds.** The policy thresholds in `router.yaml` (`min_confidence` 0.6, `upgrade_confidence` 0.75, cooldown 3, margin 0.2) stay as they are: two live turns cannot move them. The go/no-go targets are now in `router.yaml` under `review:` with the TDD's values.
