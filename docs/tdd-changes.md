| 31 | 2026-10-19 | 6 (T6.2) | Active routing order for a main request: the turn's decision once known, else its `@tier` override, else the session's current model. | 32 | 2026-10-19 | 6 (T6.2) | Fallback: a 400 or 404 on a rewritten request is retried once on the asked model; the request row records the retry (`sent_model` = asked model, `error` = `fallback: <model> returned <status>, retried on <asked>`) and the rest of the turn is not rewritten, also after a router restart (read from that row).
